About Us

RS BEAUTY SPA AND ACADEMY

RS Beauty Spa and Academy is Chatrapur's premier beauty destination, nestled in the heart of Rama Krishna Nagar. With a perfect 5-star rating backed by nearly 100 glowing reviews, we are proud to be the most trusted name in beauty, skincare, and grooming in the region. Our salon is meticulously maintained, hygienic, and designed to make every client feel pampered and at ease from the moment they walk in.

Beyond offering world-class salon services like hydra facials, bridal makeup, and skincare treatments, RS Beauty Spa and Academy is also a recognized learning institution for aspiring beauticians. Our experienced and encouraging instructors deliver hands-on training that builds real skills and lasting confidence. Whether you're here for a transformation or a career, we are committed to bringing out the best in you — at prices that are always pocket-friendly.
